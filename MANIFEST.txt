RUNTIME DEPENDENCY NOTE

Several live probes depend on the protobuf and gRPC definitions
used by the corresponding public Flint API environment.

Relevant modules include:

- api_pb2
- api_pb2_grpc

Those generated definitions are not included as private laboratory
artifacts in this repository.

The live probes are therefore environment-dependent research
scripts.

The offline evidence package can be inspected directly from the
included results/ artifacts.


PUBLICATION SCOPE
=================

The repository documents independent, read-only research into:

- observable L2/L3 stream semantics;
- liquidity and depth;
- synthetic execution capacity;
- external quote comparison;
- L2 state reconstruction;
- independent reconstruction validation;
- public maker-level information.


PRIMARY VALIDATED RESULT
========================

The strongest reconstruction validation is documented in:

results/raw/reconstruction_validation_result.json

The v4.12 validation produced:

3 validation cases
3 validated cases
3 exact matches

In the validated cases, the observable L2 book was reconstructed
from an initial snapshot plus sequential L2 updates and compared
with an independently captured reference snapshot at the
corresponding update_id.

This validates the reconstruction procedure for the tested cases.

It does not establish universal reconstruction validity across
all markets, all sessions, or all possible feed gaps.


EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
=================

The research distinguishes between:

- directly demonstrated observations;
- strongly supported observations;
- inconclusive findings;
- claims that should not be made.

The repository does not claim access to undocumented Flint
implementation.

This includes:

- undocumented matching algorithms;
- undocumented quoting algorithms;
- private maker records;
- inaccessible internal state.


MAKER INFORMATION
=================

Public L3 observations exposed a maker_id field associated with
observed book entries.

The repository does not identify the real operator behind a
maker_id.

Private maker fill and markout endpoints required organization
credentials and were not used as evidence for private fills.


SYNTHETIC EXECUTION
===================

Execution-depth and fill-ratio results are simulations over
observed public books.

They are not equivalent to real submitted orders.

They should not be interpreted as guarantees of future execution.


EXTERNAL BENCHMARK
==================

External quote comparisons are observational.

The synchronized sample was limited.

The research does not claim systematic superiority or inferiority
of Flint versus Jupiter, DFlow, or another external venue based
on this sample alone.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
========================

This repository is a curated public research package.

It is not a mirror of the complete private research laboratory.

Finite observation windows must not be interpreted as permanent
market conditions.

Synthetic execution results must not be interpreted as real
executed trades.

Public API observations must not be interpreted as proof of
undocumented internal implementation.

The v4.11 snapshot experiment is not treated as update-based
reconstruction evidence.

The v4.12 experiment is the critical update-driven reconstruction
validation.
